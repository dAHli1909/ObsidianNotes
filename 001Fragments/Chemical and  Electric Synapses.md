## Synapsis 
***Space*** between which neurons send signals 
# Chemical and  Electric Synapses

There are two basic synapses; the **electric** ones occur less frequently in our nervous system, but are the most immediate and simple types of connection. Ions such as Na+ or K+ are transmitted. 
The **chemical** synapse uses a mediator such as serotonin to give more complex instructions; sometimes, the action potential of the neuron goes along the soma and axon and on that point, it fires a protein that can be interpreted as an instruction to produce an electrical input or for any other purpose.
