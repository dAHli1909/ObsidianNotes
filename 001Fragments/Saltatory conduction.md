# Saltatory conduction
Basically, the action potential "jumps" from node to node (there are gaps between each glial cell called **Nodes of Ranvier**), skipping the myelin sheaths; this speeds the transmission of the signal

## Myelin sheaths 
Schwann cells (which basically are the myelin sheaths) are glial cells (in the **peripheral** nervous system)
In the **central** nervous system, there are oligodendrocytes
