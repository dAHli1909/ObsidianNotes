## Absolute refractory period
This is the point where the neuron cannot fire another [[ActionPotentials]], as the sodium voltage pumps have been deactivated, impeding the  Na+ ions to flow inside once again into the inside of the cell 
This prevents the action potential from traveling backwards

## Relative refractory period 
![[Pasted image 20240528182720.png]]
This is the point where, even though the sodium channels are open again, thanks to the potassium channels still pumping out K+ ions outside, it would require a larger stimulus
