# Astrocytes
They're only found in the brain and spinal cord
They intervene on the transmission of a nervous impulse between neurons
![[Pasted image 20240528190043.png]]

## Processes 
- **Recaptation of neurotransmitters** : when a neurotransmitter is no longer needed in the synapse, the astrocyte takes it off with specific proteins on its membrane. 
- **Recycling of the neurotransmitters**
- **Amplification or lessening of an impulse**
- **Regulation**: they can modify the amount of glucose and/or oxygen that a region of the bran receives via *blood* or their *reserves of glucose*
